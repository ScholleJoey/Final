Career and Background

Nick Saban is one of the most well known coaches in college football history. He spent much of his career coaching at the college and professional levels, including stops at Toledo, Michigan State, LSU, Alabama, and in the NFL. Saban became the head coach at Alabama in 2007 and remained there until his retirement from coaching in January 2024. Throughout his career, he became known for his discipline, preparation, and focus on building successful teams.

Saban began his coaching career as an assistant before getting his first head coaching job at toledo in 1990. He later became a defensive coordinator for the Cleveland Browns before returning to college football as the head coach at Michigan State. After Michigan State, he became the head coach at LSU, where he won his first national championship. He also spent time coaching the Miami Dolphins before returning to college football and taking the Alabama job. Each stop in his career helped him gain more experience and develop a coaching style he became known for.
